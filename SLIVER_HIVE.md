## Rules

- A living source grants its ability only to allied Sliver creatures. Changing
  control or leaving the battlefield updates the hive immediately. Simultaneous
  combat damage retains the existing per-step behavior and strike snapshot.
- The original four variants still grant Flying, Deathtouch, First strike and Reach.
- Multiple Prismatic sources provide the same shared **Ward (1 mana)**; it does
  not stack. A higher native Ward cost is preserved. Costs stay generic.
- New Hexproof grants invalidate opposing targets, including a previously chosen
  spell or triggered ability whose target changes before its Ward choice resolves.
- Seven new enemies are actually multicolor. Each color component matters for
  protection and removal restrictions. Receiving a keyword never changes a
  creature's colors. Existing colorless artifact enemies stay colorless.
- No Sliver or other enemy receives Indestructible.

## Ability origins

Gold broadcast symbols show abilities a creature **gives**. Blue inbound symbols
show abilities it **receives**. Keyword chips name the affected ability; hovering a
symbol names and highlights the source and recipient creatures. Small portrait
indicators summarize the links even when a long keyword list needs internal scroll.
The native shared ability appears first (after any color protection). Multiple
sources are retained in tooltips, descriptions and accessible labels. Player-controlled
Slivers and enemy choices use the same provenance. The field legend explains both symbols.

## Vey and NEW labels

Vey's edited original portrait has no monocle, fully covered leather gloves and
foil booster packs on the counter and background shelves. The left panel is slightly
wider to show the shelves and both hands. Card offers reserve space for the molten
NEW label above the face; the label and glow are not clipped by the offer. Card
descriptions keep their internal scroll, and the page remains a fixed 16:9 canvas.
