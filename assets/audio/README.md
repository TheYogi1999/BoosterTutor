# Booster opening sound

`booster-open.mp3` is an original, offline-rendered stereo Foley effect created for BoosterTutor: irregular foil crinkles and seam tearing, a pressure-release thump, air movement and seven card-slide transients. It replaces the former real-time musical chirp. No third-party samples or recordings are included. Duration: 2.15 seconds, 44.1 kHz stereo, 160 kbps MP3. Peak normalized below clipping.

## Sound suite (0.11)

`sfx-suite.mp3` contains thirteen original stereo sound cues, rendered and mastered together at 44.1 kHz / 192 kbps. Cue offsets and durations live in `sounds.js`. Category gain, a shared compressor, an eight-voice limit and short replacement fades keep overlapping effects controlled. The cues combine dry paper/metal transients, low impact bodies, air movement and warm harmonic tails; they replace the old live oscillator beeps. No third-party samples are used.

The booster Foley was remastered with high/low filtering, compression and peak limiting, and re-encoded at 192 kbps.
