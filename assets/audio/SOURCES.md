# Recorded audio sources (CC0)

- Approved receipt coin cue (v0.83): **C2 Crystal Tick** from `receipt-previews/c2-crystal-tick.mp3`; original synthesis, unchanged from its audition. Counting uses the separate effects volume/mute controls. New transition/finish previews are in [round2](receipt-previews/round2/README.md).
- Transition/count/result **auditions** (v0.82): original short designs in `receipt-previews/`, using original synthesis and the existing Kenney CC0 card/impact recordings. [Sources, generation and choice codes](receipt-previews/README.md). They are previews for player selection; no new audio family is applied to gameplay by choosing on the audition page.

- Enemy defeat sounds **S3 / M3 / L3** (v0.77): `death-previews/s3-retro-drop.mp3`, `m3-retro-defeat.mp3` and `l3-retro-colossus.mp3`. Original retro synthesis layered with **Kenney Impact Sounds (CC0)**. Sources, pinned recording mirror, fingerprints and reproducible generation are documented in [death-previews/README.md](death-previews/README.md). The selected clips retain their audition mastering and share the sound-effects mute/volume channel.

- `pack-reveal.mp3`: Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, **Pack of chips, opening**, sound 1431. A real foil/plastic packet recording, trimmed to 1.6 seconds, high/low-pass filtered, faded and mastered to -23 LUFS. https://bigsoundbank.com/pack-of-chips-opening-s1431.html — CC0.
- `card-place.ogg`, `card-slide.ogg`, `card-fan.ogg`, `card-shuffle.ogg`: Kenney **Casino Audio**, respectively `card-place-1`, `card-slide-2`, `card-fan-1`, `card-shuffle`. https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio — CC0. Original archive mirror: https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips
- `expedition-start.mp3`: Kenney **Music Jingles**, `jingles_PIZZA09`, shortened, filtered, faded and mastered to -25 LUFS. https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles — CC0. Original archive mirror: https://opengameart.org/content/85-short-music-jingles

The retained `sfx-suite.mp3` contains the project's original quieter combat/health cues. No audio was extracted from MTG Arena or a video.

- `rising-moon.mp3`: **Fantasy: Rising Moon** by RandomMind (MP3 metadata: RandomMynd), 2018. https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-rising-moon — **CC0**. Original full composition, converted to 96 kbps stereo, normalized to -22 LUFS; the final eight seconds crossfade into the opening eight seconds for a circular ambient loop. A separate music volume control defaults to 35%, with gentle fade-in, master mute and hidden-tab pause. Not audio from MTG Arena.

- `bards-tale.mp3`: **[Chiptune] Medieval: The Bard’s Tale** by RandomMind (2019), **CC0**. Source and license: https://opengameart.org/content/chiptune-medieval-the-bards-tale . Downloaded the author-provided `CHIPTUNE_Loop_The_Bards_Tale.wav`, preserving the full loop, normalized to -22 LUFS / -3 dBTP and encoded to 96 kbps stereo MP3. Replaces Rising Moon as the active soundtrack; separate volume, master mute, gesture unlock and hidden-tab pause remain.
