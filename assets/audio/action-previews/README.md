# Card and combat sound auditions

Twelve original short-effect sketches and three seven-card draw demonstrations, created for Booster Tutor. Selected for gameplay in v0.43: D1 for actual library draws, X3 for rejected actions, E3 for enemy damage and P3 for damage on the player side. Other files remain audition alternatives.

| Group | 1 · tactile | 2 · arcane | 3 · retro |
|---|---|---|---|
| D · draw | Paper Slide | Arcane Draw | Retro Flick |
| X · invalid action | Soft Stop | Arcane Denied | Retro Denied |
| E · enemy hit | Dry Impact | Arcane Impact | Retro Impact |
| P · player hit | Body Impact | Shield Pulse | Retro Damage |

The paper layers use the existing Kenney Casino Audio CC0 `card-slide.ogg`, `card-place.ogg` and `card-fan.ogg` recordings, credited in [audio sources](../SOURCES.md). Other layers are original procedural tones/noise. No audio is sampled from reference games or branded intros.

44.1 kHz stereo MP3, moderate short-effect levels, with fade envelopes and restrained high frequencies. The seven-card demos mix seven instances 160 ms apart at a fixed gain reduction so repetition can be assessed. The preview page provides a shared listening level and stops other clips when a new one starts.
