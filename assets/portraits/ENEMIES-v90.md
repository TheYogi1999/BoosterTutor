# Original art — v0.90

Two original generated fantasy illustrations were created for Booster Tutor. They
are not reused card art, game portraits or photographs. The generated source PNGs
remain in the workspace; the game uses proportional WebP conversions.

- `vey-v90.webp`, 900 × 1200: Vey, an approachable silver-haired arcane curio dealer
  in a teal coat, offering an orange booster behind a lantern-lit shop counter.
  Source: `exec-a035c1e0-5019-420e-9286-7287f2bc40bf.png`.
- `enemies-v90.webp`, 1200 × 1000: three columns and two equal rows, without card
  frames or text. Top: Rootmaw Troll, Gravebell Zombie, Galecrest Sliver.
  Bottom: Venomspine Sliver, Bladecrest Sliver, Webtendril Sliver.
  Source: `exec-2b315866-eeb1-4d9f-b950-4b488041939c.png`.

Slivers share a related serpentine silhouette, with distinct wings, venom spines,
red blades and web tendrils. All battlefield portraits retain equal display sizes.
