# Original art — v0.91

- `vey-v91.webp`, 900 × 1200, is an image-generation edit of the original v0.90
  shopkeeper portrait. Vey keeps his face, clothing and shop atmosphere; the
  monocle was removed, both hands received full leather gloves, and many sealed
  booster packs were added to the counter and shelves. Source PNG:
  `exec-fc648ad8-e91c-4658-8c41-e55b32ab3071.png`.
- `slivers-v91.webp`, 1200 × 751, is a new original generated fantasy atlas with
  four columns and two equal rows. Top, left to right: Sunbond Sliver, Quickcrest
  Sliver, Dawnwatch Sliver, Thorncharge Sliver. Bottom: Dreadcoil Sliver, Prismatic
  Sliver, Twinblade Sliver, Mistshroud Sliver. Source PNG:
  `exec-0deca2e9-6105-4c51-89b8-9f9c528e9fe7.png`.

The originals remain in the workspace. The game uses proportional WebP conversions
and equal-size battlefield portrait crops, without card frames or text baked into art.
