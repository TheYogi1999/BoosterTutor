# Enemy roster expansions

## v0.90 — 42 enemies

Six additional enemies, explicit color identities for the complete roster, Sliver
synergies and boss color protection are documented in [COLOR_PROTECTION.md](COLOR_PROTECTION.md).

## v0.89 — 36 enemies

Eight new original enemies bring the shared roster to 36 enemies. Normal encounters,
late encounter waves, Precon runs and Immortal runs all use the same roster. Existing
encounter sizes, six battlefield slots and scaling are preserved.

| Enemy | Tier | Base life / power | Keywords and behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Emberwing Imp | Skirmish | 7 / 1 | Flying, Haste; frequent attacks with an occasional recovery |
| Mirefang Toad | Skirmish | 10 / 1 | Deathtouch; recovers, attacks and eventually strengthens |
| Copperback Scarab | Elite, encounter 2+ | 22 / 3 | Artifact creature, Reach, Ward (1 mana); recovers and strengthens |
| Dawnmane Cavalier | Elite, encounter 2+ | 18 / 3 | First strike, Lifelink; alternates attacks with healing an injured ally |
| Cinderveil Phoenix | Elite, encounter 3+ | 16 / 4 | Flying, Haste; alternates attacks with a Lava Spike spell attempt |
| Thorncrown Stag | Skirmish | 13 / 3 | Reach, Trample; strengthens before attacking |
| Mooncap Myconid | Elite, encounter 3+ | 18 / 2 | Enchantment creature, Ward (1 mana); Mill 1, heal ally, attack, Shock attempt |
| Abyssal Leviathan | Boss | 66 / 6 | Trample, Ward (2 mana); recovers, attacks, rallies allies and strengthens |

Spell attempts retain the existing visible D6 check and player response windows.
Mill only moves cards from the player's Library to their graveyard. The Phoenix does
not have a resurrection ability. No new enemy has Indestructible. Color/type identity
continues to govern Doom Blade, Go for the Throat, Revoke Existence and similar spells.

Artwork and atlas order: [portrait provenance](assets/portraits/ENEMIES-v89.md).
